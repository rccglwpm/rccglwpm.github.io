Who we are

Living faith.
Local love.
Global vision.

RCCG Living Water is a Bible-believing church in Middlesbrough and part of the worldwide Redeemed Christian Church of God family.

Our public mission emphasises salvation, holiness, spiritual growth and taking the good news of Jesus Christ to people everywhere. Locally, we seek to support our community with both spiritual and practical care.
