Safeguarding

RCCG Living Water Middlesbrough is committed to making church a safe environment for children, young people and adults. Concerns about abuse, neglect, exploitation, unsafe behaviour or immediate risk should be taken seriously and handled using the church's approved safeguarding procedures.

Launch requirement: this page must not be treated as the parish's final safeguarding policy. Church leadership should add the current approved policy, named safeguarding lead or team, dedicated reporting route and emergency guidance before public launch.

Information to publish here

  • Name and church role of the designated safeguarding lead or safeguarding team.
  • A monitored safeguarding telephone number and email address.
  • How to report a concern involving a child, young person or adult at risk.
  • What someone should do where there is immediate danger or an emergency.
  • A link to the church's current approved safeguarding policy and any relevant RCCG UK policy.

General church contact

For general church enquiries, email rccglwfollowupteam@rccglwpm.org.uk or call 07904 776789. Do not present these general details as the dedicated safeguarding route unless church leadership has formally approved them for that purpose.
