Accessibility commitment

Accessibility

We want this website to be welcoming and usable by as many people as possible, regardless of device, input method or access need.

What the website supports

  • Responsive layouts for mobile, tablet and desktop screens.
  • Semantic page structure and descriptive headings.
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation, links, controls and gallery operation.
  • Visible keyboard focus indicators.
  • Alternative text for meaningful church photographs.
  • Reduced-motion support when your operating system or browser requests it.
  • A gallery with previous, next and pause/play controls instead of relying on automatic movement alone.
  • Text and controls designed to remain usable when the page is zoomed.

Animation and motion

The website uses gentle animation to make the experience feel lively. Where a visitor has enabled a reduced-motion preference, non-essential motion and automatic gallery playback are reduced or disabled.

Third-party destinations

Some actions open external services, such as EvaeXtra, YouTube, social media and Google Maps. Their accessibility is controlled by those service providers rather than this website.

Known limitations and ongoing review

Church content can change over time. New photographs, videos, forms or third-party integrations should be checked for meaningful alternative text, keyboard use, captions where appropriate, colour contrast and usable focus behaviour before publication.

Feedback

If you have difficulty using this website or need information in a different format, please contact rccglwfollowupteam@rccglwpm.org.uk or call 07904 776789.
