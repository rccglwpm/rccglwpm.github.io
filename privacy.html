Privacy & cookies

RCCG Living Water Middlesbrough respects your privacy. This notice explains how this website handles information and how to contact the church about personal data.

Information you choose to provide

If you contact the church by email or telephone, request prayer or pastoral support, or otherwise choose to get in touch, the church may receive your name, contact details and the information you include in your message.

Contact form

The contact form on this website currently opens your own email application so that you can send the message directly to the church. The website itself does not store the form submission. If a hosted form service is introduced later, this notice will need to be updated before that service is enabled.

Cookies and browser storage

This website is intentionally designed without advertising cookies, analytics cookies or persistent browser storage. The service countdown and photo gallery run locally in your browser and do not need to remember or track you. On that basis, the site does not currently display a cookie-consent banner.

If analytics, advertising technology, embedded third-party media or another feature that stores or accesses information on a visitor's device is introduced later, the church should review the cookie approach and this notice before enabling it.

External services and links

The site contains links to services operated by other organisations, including EvaeXtra for attendance check-in, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and Google Maps. These services are opened as external links rather than being embedded into this website. When you choose to visit another service, its own privacy and cookie practices apply.

Prayer and pastoral information

Prayer requests and pastoral messages can contain sensitive personal information. Please avoid including more sensitive information than is necessary. The church should limit access to appropriate authorised people and retain information only for as long as it is needed for the relevant purpose or legal obligation.

How information may be used

Information you send to the church may be used to respond to enquiries, provide requested ministry or pastoral support, administer church activities and meet applicable legal or safeguarding obligations.

Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and other uses of your personal information. Please contact the church if you have a privacy question or wish to exercise a relevant right.
